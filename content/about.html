About FursaHub

Fursa means "opportunity" in Swahili. FursaHub is a free website that helps students, graduates and young professionals across Africa find jobs, internships, scholarships, fellowships and grants they can still apply for, all in one place.

Why we started FursaHub

Talented young people in Rwanda, Kenya, Nigeria and across the continent miss opportunities every day, not because they aren't qualified, but because they never hear about them in time. Information is scattered across hundreds of websites, WhatsApp forwards are often outdated, and fake "opportunities" that ask for money are everywhere.

FursaHub was built in Rwanda to change that: one trusted place where every listing is still open, links to the official source, and is free to apply for.

What makes FursaHub different

  • Only opportunities you can still apply for. Listings are checked twice a day, and anything past its deadline, or removed by the employer, disappears.
  • Africa first. We bring together jobs from employers hiring in Rwanda, Kenya, South Africa, Nigeria and many other African countries, alongside remote and international opportunities.
  • Official sources only. Every listing links to the employer's, university's or funder's own page, where you apply directly.
  • Verified scholarships. Scholarships added by our team are checked against the official website, including the exact deadline.
  • Practical guides. From writing a CV to motivation letters and interviews, our guides help you turn an opportunity into an offer.

Our promise

  • FursaHub is free, with no account or sign-up needed.
  • We never charge you to apply, and we never ask for your bank details.
  • We don't publish opportunities that ask applicants to pay. If anyone asks you for money to apply, it is almost certainly a scam: read how to spot one.
  • We respect your privacy: what you save stays on your own device. See our Privacy Policy.

How FursaHub is funded

FursaHub is supported by advertising, which allows us to keep the site free for everyone. Advertisers have no influence over which opportunities we list.

Who we are

FursaHub is run by a small, independent team in Rwanda who care about youth employment and access to education across Africa. We are not affiliated with any of the employers, universities or funders we list.
