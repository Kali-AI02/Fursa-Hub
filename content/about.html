About FursaHub

Fursa means "opportunity" in Swahili. FursaHub brings together jobs, internships, scholarships, fellowships and grants from trusted sources, so you can find something you can still apply for without searching dozens of websites.

How it works

  • Listings are collected every day from public sources such as Grants.gov, Remotive and ReliefWeb, plus opportunities our team adds by hand.
  • When a deadline passes, the listing is removed automatically.
  • Every listing links to the official page, where you apply directly.

Our promise

  • FursaHub is free to use.
  • We never charge you to apply, and we never ask for your bank details.
  • Genuine employers and funders do not ask applicants to pay. If someone asks you to pay to apply, it is probably a scam.
