Using FursaHub

Is FursaHub free?

Yes. FursaHub is completely free for everyone, and we never charge you to apply for anything. The site is supported by advertising.

Do I need an account?

No. You can search, read and save opportunities without signing up or giving us any personal information.

How do I apply for an opportunity?

Open the opportunity and click the Apply button. It takes you to the official website of the employer, university or funder, and you apply there directly. FursaHub doesn't collect applications or take part in selection.

Where do the opportunities come from?

We collect them every day from trusted public sources: employers' own job boards (such as One Acre Fund, Zipline and GiveDirectly), Grants.gov, Remotive and others, plus opportunities our team checks and adds by hand, such as scholarships. Every listing shows its source and links to the original page.

How often is the site updated?

Twice a day. New opportunities are added, and listings whose deadline has passed, or that the employer has removed, disappear automatically.

Why do some listings say "Open until filled"?

Some employers don't publish a closing date and close the position once they've found the right person. Apply early for these.

What does "Closing soon" mean?

The deadline is within the next 7 days. "Last chance" means it closes today or tomorrow. Deadlines are shown in your own time zone, but always check the exact closing time on the official page.

Saving and sharing

How does "Save" work?

Saved opportunities are stored in your browser on this device, so you'll find them on the Saved page next time. They're not stored on our servers, and clearing your browser data removes them. Closed opportunities are removed from your list automatically.

How can I share an opportunity?

Use the Share button on any opportunity, or Share on WhatsApp on its page, to send the link to friends.

Safety

Are all listings genuine?

We only use trusted sources and link to official pages, but we can't check every detail of every listing. Always read the official page before applying, and never pay anyone to apply for a job, internship or scholarship. Read our guide on how to spot scams.

I found something suspicious or a broken link. What should I do?

Please tell us, and we'll check it and remove it if needed.

For organisations

How can I post an opportunity?

Posting is free. See Post an opportunity for what to send us. We review every submission before publishing it, and we don't publish opportunities that charge applicants a fee.

Can our organisation's jobs be added automatically?

Yes. If your organisation publishes jobs on a public careers page (for example Greenhouse, Lever or Workable), send us the link, and your open positions in Africa can appear on FursaHub automatically.

How do I ask for a listing to be removed or corrected?

Contact us with the link to the listing, and we'll update or remove it.
